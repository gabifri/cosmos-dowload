# cosmos-dowload
Web plugin for dowload all image and video on your cosmos.so board for free

Installation (Chrome, Edge, Opera, Vivaldi, Brave, etc.):
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `opera://extensions`, `edge://extensions`).
3. Enable "Developer mode."
4. Click "Load unpacked" and select the `cosmos-downloader` folder.

Usage:
1. Open your board on `cosmos.so` (works while logged in, including for private boards).
2. Click the extension icon, then select "Download all."
3. The extension will scroll to the bottom of the board and download everything to:
   Downloads/Cosmos - <board name>/001.png, 002.png, 003.mp4…

If recommended items are also being downloaded:
- Open the extension, click "Set limit manually," scroll below your board, and click on the 
  FIRST recommended image. Then, reopen the extension and click "Download all."
